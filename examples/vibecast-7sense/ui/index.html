Each call is synthesised at 32 kHz, then handed to analyze_summary for a twelve-dimensional descriptor vector, detect_segments for boundaries, compute_spectrogram for the mel view, and project_pca to place it in three dimensions. The descriptors carry different units — centroid is thousands of hertz, tonality is a ratio — so they are standardised before projection and retrieval; without that, frequency alone would dominate every distance. Position therefore reflects measured structure rather than the label: archetypes separate because they genuinely differ.

Identification is knn_search against the rest of the corpus, not a classifier. Perch emits embeddings rather than logits, and retrieval is open set — a call unlike anything indexed is reported as unknown instead of being forced into the nearest class. Votes are weighted by distance, so a few close matches outrank many mediocre ones, and the neighbours that produced each verdict are listed so it can be checked. Explained variance sits above the plot: three components never capture everything, and the figure says how much they miss.
